Drop-in DXF profiles for shop drawing details.

When one of these files is present, the generator inserts it instead of the
hand-built geometry. Delete the file to fall back to the parametric profile.

    tx380_shiplap.dxf            TX-380 shiplap joint, full section stack
    tx_tongue_and_groove.dxf     TX-450 / TX-500 tongue-and-groove stack
    panorama_section.dxf         Panorama aluminium side profile
    jamb_steel.dxf               Steel jamb plan (one side, door to the right)
    jamb_wood.dxf                Wood jamb plan (one side, door to the right)

Contract:
- Units: inches. $INSUNITS = 1.
- Origin: exterior face at the bottom of the detail. +X toward the interior.
  +Y up.
- Draw the detail at true size (panel thickness is the real thickness).
  The sheet scales the block to the view.
- Modelspace only. No paper space, no dimensions (the sheet adds leaders).
- Keep the thermal-break bars, seal, and hinge reinforcing in the geometry.
